Warm-up

5 min

Clear a bit of space and get your weights and a sturdy chair ready.

Easy march, building up5 min

March on the spot, swing your arms, then add some big shoulder circles.

Round 1

March + 3
March and punch3 min

March on the spot and punch your arms forward. Knees up to a comfortable height. You can talk, but you notice your breathing.

Goblet squat to chair10 reps

Hold the kettlebell at your chest. Sit back to a sturdy chair, then stand up tall.

Floor press10 reps

Lie on your back, knees bent, a dumbbell in each hand. Press them up from your chest, lower slowly.

Glute bridge12 reps

Lie on your back, knees bent, a dumbbell resting across your hips. Squeeze your bum and lift, pause, lower slowly.

Rest1–2 min

Have some water. Start the next round when you feel ready.

Stair step-up8 each leg

Bottom step only, holding light dumbbells, or one dumbbell and the banister. Step up, stand tall, step back down.

One-arm row10 each arm

One hand on a chair seat, feet apart. Pull the dumbbell to your hip, lower slowly.

Dead bug6 each side

Lie on your back, arms up, knees bent in the air. Slowly reach one arm back and the opposite leg out, then swap.
