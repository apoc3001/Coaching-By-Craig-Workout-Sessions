Arms · Round 1

2 + rest

One set of curls, one set of kickbacks, then rest. Three rounds.

Band for curls
Band biceps curl12–20 reps

Stand on the middle of the band, feet hip-width apart, a handle in each hand. Keep your elbows tucked into your sides, curl up, then lower slowly for 2 seconds.

Dumbbell kickback12–20 each arm

One hand on a chair or the kitchen worktop, lean forward a little with a flat back. Elbow up by your side, straighten your arm back, squeeze, then lower slowly. Both arms, left first.

Rest60 sec

Have some water, then start the next round.

Glutes · Round 1

2 + rest

One set of bridges, the leg raises on both sides, then rest. Three rounds.

Kettlebell glute bridge15–20 reps

Lie on your back, knees bent, feet flat, kettlebell held on your hips. Push through your heels and squeeze your bum to lift. Hold 2 seconds at the top, lower slowly. Keep your ribs down so your lower back doesn't arch. If your right knee aches, move your feet a little further away.

Side-lying leg raise12–15 each side

Lie on your side, bottom knee bent, top leg straight and slightly behind you, toes pointing forward. Lift the top leg about a hand's height, pause, lower slowly. You should feel it at the side of your bum, not the front of your hip.

Rest60 sec

Have some water, then start the next round.

Shoulders

2 sets

Slow and controlled. This one builds up your shoulders, especially your left.

Lying Y raise10 reps

Lie face down, forehead on a folded towel, arms overhead in a Y with thumbs up. Lift both arms a few centimetres off the floor, hold 2 seconds, lower. No weight needed. If your left shoulder hurts, stop this one and tell Craig.

Rest45 sec

How hard should it feel?

Stop each set when you could only do about 2 more good reps.

6+ more?Make it harder

Form going?Make it easier

When you hit the top of the rep range on every set, make it harder next time:

  • Curls · stand with your feet wider on the band, or swap to the heavier band
  • Kickbacks · take 3 seconds to lower
  • Glute bridge · hold the top for 3 seconds
  • Leg raise · pause for 2 seconds at the top
